Cast Menu to TV

Launch the Snack Station digital menu board directly on any Chromecast connected to your Wi-Fi.

    Searching for Chromecast on Wi-Fi...
    • Pick the TV: after you click Cast, Chrome lists the TVs. Choose one. Repeat for each TV with its own menu.
    • This PC does the work: keep this page open and the PC awake and on the store Wi-Fi. If the PC sleeps, the TVs go back to their photo screen.
    • Fills the screen: the menu is drawn at the TV's own size, so there are no black bars.
    • Keeps running on its own: Once cast, you can close this page or turn off your phone. The Chromecast keeps showing the menu.
    • After a power cut or long Wi-Fi outage: the Chromecast goes back to its home screen. Open this page and cast again.
    • Same Wi-Fi required: Make sure this device and the Chromecast are connected to the same store Wi-Fi network.
