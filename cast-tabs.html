Cast the menus from this PC

Each button opens one TV's menu in a window shaped like a TV, so the cast fills the whole screen.

Left TVPizza & Wings · cast to "Left Tv Menu TV"
Center TVPlatters & Favorites · cast to "Center tv Menu TV"
Right TVSmoothies & Healthy · cast to "Right Tv Menu TV"
  1. Click a TV's button. A menu window opens.
  2. Right-click inside that window and choose Cast…, then pick that TV.
  3. Repeat for the other two TVs. Keep the windows open (they can sit behind each other).

If a size line turns red, the window didn't fit on this screen: pick a smaller size and open it again.
